Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PS: Portable Scanner & PDF ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Processing & Local Storage

We prioritize user privacy. PS: Portable Scanner & PDF operates primarily as an on-device utility:

  • Local Processing: All image scanning, optical processing, page management, and PDF generation occur strictly locally on your device.
  • No Cloud Storage: Your scanned documents, images, and converted files are not transmitted, backed up, or stored on any external servers operated by us.

2. Data Security & Encryption

The App provides built-in protection features to secure your content:

  • PIN Protection & Local Encryption: Any PIN codes, secured image containers, and internal application security settings are stored encrypted exclusively on your local device storage.
  • Data Control: You retain complete ownership and control over your files, including the ability to export, encrypt, or delete them at any time.

3. Device Permissions

To provide core functionalities, the App may request access to specific device features:

  • Camera: Required solely to allow you to take photos of physical documents directly within the app for scanning and PDF conversion.
  • Storage / Media Access: Required to save exported PDF files, store encrypted images, and import existing images from your gallery.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Services

To keep the App free to use, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Third-party ad networks may collect and process non-personally identifiable information, such as:

  • Advertising ID (AAID)
  • IP address and coarse location data
  • App performance, diagnostics, and crash logs

For more information on how Google manages advertising data, please review the Google Privacy & Terms Policy.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in app features or legal requirements. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
