Privacy Policy for PS: Portable Scanner & PDF

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information Collection and Use

PS: Portable Scanner & PDF processes images and document files locally on your device. We do not store or transmit your scanned images to external servers.

2. Data Security & Encryption

All app-level lock settings and image protections (such as PIN security) are maintained strictly on your local device storage.

3. Advertising & Analytics

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers to serve non-personalized or personalized ads in accordance with Google's policies.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us via developer support.
